1/3 Cup to Grams: Exact Kitchen Conversion Guide

Converting recipe measurements when baking can be confusing, especially when working with fractional cups. One of the most frequently searched culinary conversions is 1/3 cup to grams.

Because cups measure volume and grams measure weight (mass), the exact gram equivalent of 1/3 cup depends on the density of the ingredient you are measuring.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How many grams is 1/3 cup of liquid?

1/3 cup of liquid (such as water, milk, or juice) equals 78.86 grams (commonly rounded to 79 grams or 2.67 US fluid ounces) because water has a standard density of 1 gram per milliliter.

Q2: How many tablespoons are in 1/3 cup?

There are exactly 5.33 tablespoons (or 5 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon) in 1/3 US cup.

Q3: Why is 1/3 cup of flour lighter than 1/3 cup of sugar?

Grams measure weight (mass) while cups measure volume. All-purpose flour is fluffy and aerated, weighing only 40 grams per 1/3 cup, whereas granulated white sugar is dense and crystalline, weighing 67 grams per 1/3 cup.
