Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

1. Overview

At OmniConverter, accessible from https://omniconverter.co.uk, user privacy is one of our primary priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information processed and how it is handled.

2. Client-Side Data Processing

OmniConverter operates entirely as a client-side web application. All unit mathematical calculations (Temperature, Weight, Volume, Time, Area, Speed) and file format conversions (Images, Documents, Data) are executed locally inside your web browser using HTML5 JavaScript, Canvas, and Blob APIs. Your files and data are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party database.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We do not use tracking cookies to collect personally identifiable information (PII). Standard non-personally identifiable log files may record basic web server metrics (such as browser type, IP address, and page visit count) for security and performance optimization.

4. Third-Party Services

OmniConverter does not share, sell, or rent any user data to third-party advertising or marketing services.

5. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at info.omniconverter@gmail.com or via our Contact Page.

Privacy Policy & Data Security Commitment

At OmniConverter, we believe user privacy is a fundamental standard. This Privacy Policy details our strict client-side data architecture and minimal analytics practices.

1. Zero File Upload Policy

When using our File & Media Converter tools (including PNG, JPEG, WebP, JSON, CSV format operations), all processing is performed locally inside your device web browser memory using HTML5 APIs. Your uploaded files are never transmitted across network servers or stored on remote databases.

2. Analytics & Traffic Measurement

We utilize Google Analytics (GA4) and Ahrefs Webmaster verification scripts to measure generalized web traffic performance, browser compatibility metrics, and user navigation paths. No personally identifiable information (PII) is sold or shared.

3. Contact Information

For privacy questions or data inquiries, contact us at info.omniconverter@gmail.com.
