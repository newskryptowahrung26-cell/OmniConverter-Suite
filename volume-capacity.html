Volume and Capacity Measurement Guide

Volume calculations determine three-dimensional space capacity. Across culinary baking, fluid mechanics, beverage distribution, automotive engine displacement, and pool maintenance, converting between US customary liquid units and metric capacity standards is essential.

Key Volume Conversion Multipliers

  • 1 Liter (L) = 1,000 Milliliters (mL) = 33.814 fluid ounces (fl oz)
  • 1 US Liquid Gallon = 3.78541 Liters = 128 US fluid ounces
  • 1 Imperial UK Gallon = 4.54609 Liters = 160 UK fluid ounces
  • 1 US Cup = 236.588 Milliliters = 8 US fluid ounces
  • 1 Metric Cup = 250 Milliliters

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How many mL in a cup?
A: A standard US customary cup equals 236.588 mL, while a standard metric cup equals 250 mL.

Q: Why is a UK gallon different from a US gallon?
A: The US gallon is based on the historic 231 cubic inch wine gallon, whereas the UK Imperial gallon is based on 10 pounds of water volume.
