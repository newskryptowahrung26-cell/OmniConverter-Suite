Frequently Asked Questions About UK Salary and Taxes

How much tax do I pay on a £35,000 salary in the UK?

For England, Wales, and Northern Ireland (Rest of UK), you receive a tax-free Personal Allowance of £12,570. The remaining £22,430 is taxed at the 20% basic rate (£4,486 income tax) plus 8% employee National Insurance Category A (£1,794.40).

What is the £100,000 Personal Allowance taper?

For adjusted net income exceeding £100,000, your tax-free Personal Allowance decreases by £1 for every £2 earned, creating an effective 60% marginal income tax rate between £100,000 and £125,140.
