Frequently Asked Questions About BMI and Healthy Weight

How is BMI calculated in metric and imperial?

Metric formula: Weight (kg) ÷ [Height (m)]². Imperial formula: [Weight (lbs) × 703] ÷ [Height (inches)]².

What are the official WHO BMI categories?

Underweight is below 18.5, Normal weight is 18.5–24.9, Overweight is 25.0–29.9, and Obese is 30.0 or higher.
