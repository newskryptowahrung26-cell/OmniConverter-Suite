Frequently Asked Questions About Compound Interest

What is the formula for compound interest with monthly deposits?

The future value formula combines initial principal compounding: P(1 + r/n)^(nt) with the future value of a series of regular deposits: PMT [((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)].

What is the Rule of 72?

The Rule of 72 is a mental math shortcut to estimate how many years it takes for an investment to double: divide 72 by the annual interest rate (e.g., at 8%, money doubles in ~9 years).
