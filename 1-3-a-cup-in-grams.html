How Much is 1 3 a Cup in Grams? Kitchen Conversion Guide

Converting recipe measurements when baking can be confusing, especially when working with fractional cups. One of the most frequently searched culinary conversions is 1 3 a cup in grams (1/3 cup in grams).

Because cups measure volume and grams measure weight (mass), the exact gram equivalent of 1/3 cup depends on the density of the ingredient you are measuring.

Why Does 1/3 Cup Weight Change by Ingredient?

Volume (cups) tells you how much space an ingredient occupies, whereas mass (grams) tells you how heavy it is. Aerated, powdery ingredients like flour are much lighter per cup than dense liquids like honey or syrup.
