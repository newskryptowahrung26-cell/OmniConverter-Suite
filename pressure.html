Bar in to PSI: Conversion Formula, Steps & Gauge Pressure

Converting bar in to psi (or bar a psi conversion) is one of the most frequent calculations in automotive care, pneumatic machinery, HVAC, and industrial engineering. While bar is widely used throughout the UK, continental Europe, and international metric standards, psi (pounds per square inch) remains the standard measure in the United States and classic automotive guides.

Direct Conversion Formula

Pressure in PSI = Pressure in Bar × 14.503774

Reverse Formula: Pressure in Bar = Pressure in PSI ÷ 14.503774 (or × 0.0689476)

Barg to PSI & Bar to PSIG: Gauge vs. Absolute Pressure Explained

In process engineering, oil & gas, steam generation, and industrial hydraulics, distinguishing between gauge pressure (barg, psig) and absolute pressure (bar(a), psia) is vital to avoid catastrophic piping calculation mistakes:

The Thermodynamic Equations

  • Pabsolute = Pgauge + Patmospheric
  • At standard sea level, Patmospheric = 1.01325 bar (101.325 kPa or 14.6959 psi).
  • 1 barg (bar gauge) indicates pressure reading 1 bar above ambient air. In absolute terms, 1 barg = 2.01325 bar(a).
  • 1 barg to psig: Because both barg and psig are referenced to ambient zero, 1 barg = 14.5038 psig directly.
  • To convert barg to psia: Pressure (psia) = (barg × 14.5038) + 14.6959.

Frequently Asked Questions & Practical Applications

How do I convert 220, 230, and 250 kPa to psi for tyre inflation?
Divide kilopascals by 6.894757. 220 kPa equals 31.91 psi (round to 32 psi on mechanical gauges). 230 kPa equals 33.36 psi (round to 33.5 psi). 250 kPa equals 36.26 psi (round to 36.5 psi). Always check pressures when tyres are cold for maximum safety.

What is the difference between bar, barg, and psig?
Bar is an absolute pressure unit unless qualified. Barg refers specifically to gauge pressure (bar gauge) above atmospheric pressure. Psig is pounds per square inch gauge. Because both barg and psig place zero at atmospheric pressure, converting between them is direct: 1 barg = 14.5038 psig.

How do I convert bar in to psi manually?
Multiply the bar reading by 14.5. For quick mental estimates: 2 bar is ~29 psi, 2.2 bar is ~32 psi, and 2.5 bar is ~36 psi. For exact scientific requirements, multiply by 14.503774.

Why do scientific institutions specify Pascal (Pa)?
The Pascal is the SI derived unit of pressure defined by the BIPM and NIST as 1 newton per square metre. 1 bar is exactly 100,000 Pascals (100 kPa), designed to approximate Earth's atmospheric pressure while keeping clean decimal metric scaling.
