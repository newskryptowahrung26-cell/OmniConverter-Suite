1 Cup Milk in Milliliters: Exact Baking Guide

Measuring liquid ingredients accurately is essential when baking cakes, breads, or custards. A frequent question in culinary conversions is 1 cup milk in milliliters.

In US Customary recipes, 1 cup of milk equals 236.59 mL (rounded to 240 mL in standard American baking). In Metric recipes (Australia, NZ, UK), 1 Metric cup equals 250 mL.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How many mL is 1 cup of milk?

1 US customary cup of milk equals 236.59 mL (commonly rounded to 240 mL). A Metric cup equals 250 mL.

Q2: How many grams is 1 cup of milk?

Whole milk has a density of ~1.03 g/mL, so 1 US cup of milk weighs approximately 245 grams.

Q3: How many fluid ounces are in 1 cup of milk?

There are exactly 8 US fluid ounces (fl oz) in 1 US cup of milk.
