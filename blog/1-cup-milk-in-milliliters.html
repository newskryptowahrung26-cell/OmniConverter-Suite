Culinary Volume Guide: 1 Cup Milk in mL

1 Cup Milk in Milliliters: Exact Fluid Volume Guide

Whether you are baking a delicate cake batter, crafting a creamy bechamel sauce, or preparing morning pancakes, precise liquid measurement is essential for culinary success. A fundamental kitchen question that home cooks and pastry chefs frequently ask is: 1 cup milk in milliliters is how much?

Quick Conversion Answer

1 US Customary Cup of Milk = 236.59 mL (rounded to 237 mL or 240 mL)
1 Metric Cup of Milk (UK, Australia, New Zealand, Canada) = 250.00 mL
1 Imperial Cup of Milk (Legacy British) = 284.13 mL

Understanding Liquid Volume vs Mass for Milk

Because milliliters (mL) measure volumetric displacement and grams (g) measure mass, converting milk involves analyzing fluid density. Water has a standard density of 1.00 g/mL at room temperature, meaning 1 mL = 1 gram.

Whole milk, however, contains natural milk fats, dissolved proteins (casein, whey), and lactose sugars, giving it a slightly higher average density of 1.03 grams per milliliter (1.03 g/mL).

Weight of 1 US Cup Milk = 236.588 mL x 1.03 g/mL = 243.68 grams

Why Regional Cup Differences Matter in Baking

If you are attempting a recipe published in the UK, Australia, or New Zealand using an American 236.59 mL cup, you will be adding about 13.4 mL less milk per cup than the recipe author intended.

In cake batters or bread doughs, a shortfall of 13.4 mL of milk can result in dry, crumbly cakes or dense yeast loaves. Conversely, if you use a 250 mL metric cup for a US recipe, your batter may end up overly wet. Always check the source country of your recipe before pouring!

Practical Kitchen Tips for Measuring Milk

  1. Use Liquid Measuring Jugs: Always pour milk into a clear glass or plastic liquid measuring jug resting flat on your counter. Never hold the measuring cup in your hand in mid air!
  2. Check Eye Level: Look at the liquid level curve at eye level to verify the liquid line rests precisely on the target milliliter graduation mark.
  3. Tare Your Digital Scale: Place your mixing bowl on a digital scale, hit Tare to zero out weight, change units to grams or mL, and pour milk directly until reaching 244g for 1 US cup of whole milk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 1 cup of milk 240 mL or 250 mL?

In the US, 1 cup equals 236.59 mL (rounded to 240 mL on nutrition labels). In Australia, UK, and New Zealand, 1 metric cup equals 250 mL.

How many grams is 1 cup of milk?

1 US cup of whole milk weighs 243.7 grams because milk density is slightly higher (1.03 g/mL) than water (1.00 g/mL).

How many tablespoons of milk are in 1 cup?

There are 16 US tablespoons in 1 US cup of milk.
