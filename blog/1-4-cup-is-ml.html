1 4 Cup is mL: Exact Kitchen & Baking Guide

Whether you are following an international recipe or baking at home, knowing exactly how much 1 4 cup is mL (1/4 cup in milliliters) is essential for culinary precision. Because liquid measuring cups and recipe standards vary worldwide, small volume differences can alter your dough consistency or sauce balance.

In standard US Customary recipes, 1/4 cup is equal to 59.15 mL (commonly rounded to 60 mL for convenient kitchen measuring).

How to Convert 1/4 Cup to mL for Common Ingredients

Milliliters (mL) measure liquid volume. For liquid ingredients like water, milk, oil, and vinegar, 1/4 cup always equals approximately 60 mL. When converting dry ingredients by volume, 1/4 cup occupies 60 mL of space, but the weight in grams will differ depending on ingredient density.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How many mL is 1/4 cup of liquid?

1/4 US customary cup of liquid equals 59.15 mL, standardly rounded to 60 mL in recipes.

Q2: How many tablespoons are in 1/4 cup?

There are exactly 4 US tablespoons (or 12 US teaspoons) in a 1/4 cup.

Q3: What is the difference between US 1/4 cup and Metric 1/4 cup in mL?

A US 1/4 cup is 59.15 mL, whereas a Metric 1/4 cup (common in Australia & New Zealand) is exactly 62.5 mL.
