1/4 Cup is How Many mL? Exact Liquid & Kitchen Volume Guide

✍️ Author: Chef Claire Hastings 🔬 Reviewed by: Sarah Jenkins, MSc 📅 Last Reviewed: October 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read

When preparing liquid ingredients, salad dressings, sauces, or baking recipes, converting volumetric measurements accurately is critical. One of the most essential kitchen volume questions asked worldwide is: 1/4 cup is how many mL?

Quick Conversion Answer

1/4 US Customary Cup = 59.15 mL (commonly rounded to 59 mL or 60 mL in kitchen practice)
1/4 Metric Cup (UK, Australia, New Zealand, Canada) = 62.50 mL
1/4 Imperial Cup (Legacy UK) = 71.03 mL
1/4 US Legal Cup (Nutrition Labels) = 60.00 mL
1/4 Japanese Cup = 50.00 mL

Understanding Milliliters vs Cups

A milliliter (mL) is a standardized metric unit of liquid volume defined as one thousandth of a liter (1 mL = 0.001 L). Because the metric system is decimal based, milliliters provide a precise measurement used globally in scientific laboratories, medicine, and international cookery.

Conversely, a cup is a traditional unit of volume widely used in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand. Depending on the country of origin, the exact milliliter value of a cup changes slightly. In US recipes, 1 US Customary Cup equals 236.588 mL, which makes 1/4 cup equal to 59.147 mL.

Step-by-Step Mathematical Calculation

Converting fractional cups to milliliters is straightforward once you know the conversion factor of the target standard:

Volume in mL = Volume in Cups x 236.588 mL per Cup

Plugging in 1/4 or 0.25:

Volume in mL = 0.25 x 236.588 = 59.147 mL

For everyday cooking and baking in North America, rounding 59.15 mL to 60 mL is universally accepted and will not noticeably affect recipe outcomes.

Practical Kitchen Breakdown: Tablespoons and Teaspoons

If you don't have a 1/4 measuring cup on hand, you can easily measure out 1/4 cup using standard kitchen spoons:

  • 1/4 Cup = 4 Tablespoons: 1 US tablespoon equals 14.79 mL. 4 x 14.79 mL = 59.15 mL.
  • 1/4 Cup = 12 Teaspoons: 1 US teaspoon equals 4.93 mL. 12 x 4.93 mL = 59.15 mL.
  • 1/4 Cup = 2 US Fluid Ounces: 1 US fluid ounce equals 29.57 mL. 2 x 29.57 mL = 59.15 mL.

Liquid vs Dry Ingredients: What You Must Know

A milliliter is strictly a measure of volume or space occupied. Therefore, 1/4 cup of any pure liquid whether water, vegetable oil, milk, lemon juice, or heavy cream will always occupy approximately 59.15 mL of volume.

However, if you attempt to measure weight in grams, the answer changes dramatically based on density:

  • 1/4 cup of Water: 59.15 mL = 59.15 grams (density = 1.00 g/mL)
  • 1/4 cup of Honey: 59.15 mL = 84.00 grams (density = 1.42 g/mL)
  • 1/4 cup of Olive Oil: 59.15 mL = 54.40 grams (density = 0.92 g/mL)
  • 1/4 cup of All-Purpose Flour: 59.15 mL = 30.00 grams (fluffy dry powder)

Common Kitchen Applications for 1/4 Cup

1/4 cup (59 mL) is one of the most frequently used sub-measurements in culinary arts:

  1. Salad Vinaigrettes: A standard single batch of dressing often calls for 1/4 cup of extra virgin olive oil mixed with 2 tablespoons of balsamic vinegar.
  2. Pancake Batter: Scoop 1/4 cup (59 mL) of batter per pancake onto a hot griddle for perfectly uniform 4-inch silver-dollar pancakes.
  3. Coffee Creamer: Adding 1/4 cup of milk or heavy cream to a mug of coffee adds roughly 59 mL of dairy richness.
  4. Baking Leavening Agents: Measuring out 1/4 cup of melted butter or buttermilk ensures tender crumbs in muffins and biscuits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 1/4 cup equal to 50 mL or 60 mL?

1/4 US Customary Cup is 59.15 mL, which is commonly rounded to 60 mL in recipes. In Japan, 1/4 cup equals 50 mL.

How many tablespoons make 1/4 cup?

There are exactly 4 US tablespoons in 1/4 US cup.

How many fluid ounces is 1/4 cup?

1/4 US cup equals exactly 2 US fluid ounces.

Whether you are following an American cookbook, a European baking recipe, or an Australian food blog, knowing that 1/4 US cup equals 59.15 mL (rounded to 60 mL) makes converting liquid measurements instant and hassle free.
