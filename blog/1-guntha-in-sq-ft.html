1 Guntha in Square Feet (Land Measurement Guide)

In Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat, Goa, and Telangana, the Guntha (गुंठा) is the standard legal revenue unit for residential and agricultural plots.

Quick Summary Answer

1 Guntha = Exactly 1,089 Square Feet (sq ft)

40 Gunthas equal exactly 1 Acre (43,560 sq ft). 1 Guntha also equals 121 Square Yards (Gaj).

The Exact Mathematical Formula: 1 Guntha in Sq Ft

In Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana, land revenue records (7/12 extracts and RTCs) express agricultural plots in Guntha (गुंठा):

1 Guntha = Exactly 1,089 Square Feet
1 Guntha = 121 Square Yards
1 Guntha = 101.17 Square Meters
40 Gunthas = Exactly 1 Acre (43,560 sq ft)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is 1 Guntha equal to 1 Are?

Yes, in metric land surveying, 1 Guntha (101.17 sq m) is practically equivalent to 1 Are (100 sq m), differing by only 1.17% due to rounding.

How many Gunthas make 1 Hectare?

One hectare contains approximately 98.84 Gunthas (roughly 2.47 acres).
