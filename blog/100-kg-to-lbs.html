To convert any mass from kilograms to pounds, multiply the kilogram figure by the international avoirdupois pound conversion constant of 2.20462262:

Weight in LBS = Weight in KG x 2.20462262

Step-by-Step Calculation for 100 Kilograms:

  1. Take the base mass: 100 kg.
  2. Multiply by the pound constant: 100 x 2.20462262 = 220.462262 lbs.
  3. Rounding to two decimal places yields 220.46 pounds.
