Understanding Teaspoon Standards Around the World

A milliliter (mL) is a standardized metric unit of volume equal to 0.001 liters. Because the metric system is decimal based, milliliters provide precision across cooking, pharmacology, and chemistry. Depending on where your recipe or medical dosage instructions originated, the exact volume of 1 teaspoon varies:

  • US Customary Teaspoon: Equal to 1/3 tablespoon, 1/6 fluid ounce, or 4.92892 mL.
  • Metric Teaspoon: Standardized across Australia, New Zealand, the UK, and Canada as exactly 5.00 mL.
  • Medical Dosing Teaspoon: Standardized in pharmacology as exactly 5 mL to prevent medication dosing errors.

Step-by-Step Mathematical Formula

To convert any fractional teaspoon measurement into milliliters, multiply the teaspoon value by 4.92892:

Volume in mL = Teaspoons x 4.92892 mL per Teaspoon

For everyday kitchen cooking and baking, rounding 4.93 mL to 5 mL is universally accepted across recipe books. However, when dosing pediatric liquid medication, always use the calibrated oral syringe provided by your pharmacy rather than a household eating spoon.

Liquid vs Dry Teaspoon Measurements

When measuring liquid ingredients like olive oil, honey, or vanilla extract, the liquid fills the volume completely. However, when measuring dry powdered spices like baking powder, cinnamon, or salt:

  • Level Teaspoon: Sweep the top flat with a knife for exact volume (4.93 mL).
  • Heaping Teaspoon: Creates a rounded mound that can double the weight of the ingredient (up to 8 or 10 mL). Always use level teaspoons in baking!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 1 teaspoon equal to 5 mL?

1 US teaspoon equals 4.93 mL, which is commonly rounded to 5 mL in kitchen cooking. In the UK, Australia, and New Zealand, 1 metric teaspoon is defined as exactly 5.00 mL.

How many teaspoons make 1 tablespoon?

There are exactly 3 teaspoons in 1 tablespoon.

How many teaspoons are in 1 fluid ounce?

There are 6 US teaspoons in 1 US fluid ounce.
