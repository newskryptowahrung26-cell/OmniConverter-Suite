1 Gaj in Square Feet and Square Yards Guide

In Northern and Central India (Delhi-NCR, Haryana, Punjab, UP), property dealers and plot registries quote land sizes in Gaj (गज).

Quick Summary Answer

1 Gaj = Exactly 9 Square Feet (1 Square Yard)

A standard 100 Gaj plot equals exactly 900 square feet (or 83.61 square meters).

The Exact Mathematical Formula: 1 Gaj in Sq Ft

A Gaj (गज) is traditionally defined in Indian revenue law as exactly 1 linear yard (3 linear feet or 36 inches). Because land is an area measurement, 1 square Gaj equals 1 square yard:

Area in Square Feet = Total Gaj × 9
Area in Square Meters = Total Gaj × 0.836127

This means when you purchase a 100 Gaj plot in Gurgaon, Noida, or Delhi, you are acquiring exactly 900 square feet (or 83.61 square meters).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is 1 Gaj equal to 1 Gaz?

Yes, "Gaj" and "Gaz" are spelling variations of the same traditional Hindi/Urdu land measurement unit, universally standardized as 9 square feet.

How many Gaj are in 1 Bigha?

In Uttar Pradesh and Delhi-NCR, a standard Pucca Bigha equals approximately 3,025 Gaj (27,225 sq ft), whereas a Kachha Bigha is about 1,008 Gaj.
