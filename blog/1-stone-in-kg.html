Defining Stone and Kilograms

To understand how stone converts to kilograms:

  • Stone (st): An Imperial unit of mass historically used in Great Britain and Ireland for trade and human body weight. Defined as exactly 14 avoirdupois pounds.
  • Kilogram (kg): The base unit of mass in the International System of Units (SI), defined by fixing the numerical value of the Planck constant.

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

Since 1 pound equals 0.45359237 kg, multiplying by 14 gives:

1 Stone = 14 x 0.45359237 = 6.35029318 Kilograms

To convert any weight in stone and pounds to kilograms:

  1. Multiply the stone number by 6.35029.
  2. Multiply any remaining pounds by 0.45359.
  3. Add the two results together to get total kilograms.

Historical Origin of the Stone Measurement Unit

The stone has been used since antiquity in England and Northern Europe for agricultural trade. Historically, a stone varied depending on what commodity was being weighed (for example, a stone of wool was 14 or 15 pounds, whereas a stone of beef was 8 pounds). In 1835, the British Parliament standardized the stone to exactly 14 Imperial pounds.
