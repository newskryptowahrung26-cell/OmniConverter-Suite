Volume & Capacity Guide: 500 mL to Cups

500 mL to Cups: Liquid Volume Conversion

Converting 500 mL to cups is essential when following international liquid recipes. 500 mL equals 2.11 US cups (or 2 metric cups).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How many cups is 500 mL?

500 mL equals 2.11 US Customary Cups or exactly 2.00 Metric Cups (used in UK, Australia, New Zealand, and Canada).

Is 500 mL half a liter?

Yes! 500 mL is exactly equal to 0.5 liters.

How many fluid ounces is 500 mL?

500 mL equals approximately 16.91 US fluid ounces.
