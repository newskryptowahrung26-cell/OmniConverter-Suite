Overview of the Official Currency of Indonesia (Indonesian Rupiah - IDR)

The official currency of the Republic of Indonesia is the Indonesian Rupiah, represented internationally by the ISO 4217 currency code IDR and domestically by the symbol Rp. Issued and regulated by Bank Indonesia (the central bank of Indonesia), the Rupiah is the sole legal tender across the entire Indonesian archipelago, including Bali, Jakarta, Lombok, Java, and Sumatra. Because the Indonesian Rupiah trades at thousands of units per US Dollar, Euro, or Australian Dollar (typically around Rp 15,500–16,200 per 1 USD and Rp 10,000–10,600 per 1 AUD), visitors quickly become "instant millionaires" when withdrawing cash from local ATMs. Understanding how to read Rupiah prices, distinguish banknote colors, and avoid hidden conversion markups is essential for travelers, remote workers, and international importers.

Bank Indonesia circulates seven main paper/polymer banknote denominations from Rp 1,000 to Rp 100,000, alongside aluminum and nickel-plated coins from Rp 100 to Rp 1,000. In restaurants and retail shops across Bali and Jakarta, menus frequently omit the last three zeros—writing "75k" or "75" to indicate Rp 75,000.

Mental Math Shortcut for Converting IDR on the Go

When shopping in local markets or checking ride-hailing fares on Grab or Gojek, use these quick mental conversion rules:

  • IDR to Australian Dollars (AUD): Drop the last four zeros. For example, Rp 100,000 is roughly A$10 AUD (exact mid-market value ≈ A$9.65 AUD), and Rp 50,000 is roughly A$5 AUD.
  • IDR to US Dollars (USD): Divide the thousands value by 16 (or multiply the 100k figure by 6.3). For example, Rp 100,000 ≈ $6.33 USD, and Rp 1,000,000 (1 million Rupiah) ≈ $63.29 USD.
  • IDR to British Pounds (GBP): Divide by 20,000. Thus Rp 100,000 ≈ £5.00 GBP and Rp 1,000,000 ≈ £50.00 GBP.

Cash, ATMs, and QRIS Digital Payments in Indonesia

Indonesia blends modern cashless infrastructure in major cities and tourist hubs with cash-only commerce in rural areas and smaller islands:

  • Authorized Money Changers: If exchanging physical USD, AUD, or EUR banknotes in Bali or Jakarta, only use official money changers displaying the green PVA Berizin (Bank Indonesia Authorized) shield. Note that Indonesian banks require USD $100 bills to be crisp, post-2006 series notes without stamps, tears, or heavy creases.
  • ATM Withdrawals & Denominations: Stick to ATMs physically attached to major bank branches such as Bank Mandiri, BCA, BNI, or BRI. Look at the sticker on the ATM machine indicating whether it dispenses Rp 50,000 or Rp 100,000 notes; most machines limit single withdrawals to 25–30 notes (Rp 1,250,000 to Rp 3,000,000 per transaction).
  • Always Decline Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC): When paying by card or withdrawing from an ATM, always choose to be charged in local currency (IDR) rather than your home currency (USD, AUD, GBP, EUR) to avoid 4%–7% terminal conversion markups.
  • QRIS Contactless Payments: Bank Indonesia's national QR code standard (QRIS) is accepted everywhere from luxury hotels to street food stalls (warungs). International visitors can link select regional e-wallets or multi-currency travel cards to pay via QRIS.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I pay with US Dollars or Australian Dollars directly in Bali?

No. Under Bank Indonesia Regulation No. 17/3/PBI/2015, all domestic retail transactions, hotel bills, and tour bookings within Indonesia must be priced and settled in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). Always convert your foreign currency or withdraw IDR from an official ATM.

Why do Rp 100,000 and Rp 10,000 notes look similar in low light?

The Rp 100,000 note is reddish-pink while the Rp 10,000 note is purple-magenta, and the Rp 20,000 note is green while the Rp 2,000 note is grey. Always count the zeros carefully in dimly lit taxis or night markets before handing over cash.

How much is 1 Million Indonesian Rupiah in USD and AUD?

At typical mid-market reference rates, Rp 1,000,000 (one million IDR, called "satu juta" in Indonesian) equals approximately $63.29 USD or A$96.53 AUD.
