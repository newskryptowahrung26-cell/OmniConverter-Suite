How do I convert 1/3 cup to grams for baking?

1/3 cup weight depends on ingredient density: Water is ~79 grams, all-purpose flour is 40 grams, white sugar is 67 grams, brown sugar is 73 grams, and unsalted butter is 76 grams. Read our full 1/3 Cup in Grams Baking Guide.
